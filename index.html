The Traditional Process

From Milk to Golden Ghee

We stay true to our roots by churning our ghee the traditional way — six steps that have not changed in generations of South Indian kitchens.

01

Milk

Fresh cow's milk, gathered at the start of the day.

02

Curd

The milk is set overnight and left to turn.

03

Churning

Slow-churned with a wooden mathu, by hand, in rhythm.

04

Butter

Butter gathers and rises to the surface.

05

Slow Heating

Heated slowly in a heavy vessel, unhurried.

06

Golden Ghee

What remains is clear, golden and aromatic.

This ancient, slow-churning technique preserves the natural, rich aroma of the milk. It is what gives SATVA its deeply flavourful character and authentically granular texture — something industrial methods simply cannot replicate.
